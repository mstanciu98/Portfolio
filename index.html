About Me

Engineering at the intersection of mechanism, robotics, and manufacturing.

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Brigham Young University with a focus on design, robotics, and optimization. My work spans surgical device development, NASA-funded robotics research, autonomous rover systems, and software-driven design tools.

I'm drawn to problems that require tight iteration loops — going from sketch to prototype to tested hardware as quickly and rigorously as possible. Whether it's a single-use surgical clip or a 21-unit inflatable robot roller, I care about the details that make the difference between a part that works in the lab and one that works in the field.
