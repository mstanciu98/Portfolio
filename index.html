About Me

Engineering at the intersection of design, product development, and manufacturing.

I’m a mechanical engineering graduate student at Brigham Young University completing my M.S. degree in August 2026, focusing on mechanical design, product development, and rapid prototyping. My experience spans surgical devices, robotic systems, and manufacturing-driven design projects, with an emphasis on translating concepts into precise, manufacturable hardware through iterative design, fabrication, testing, and refinement.

I enjoy engineering challenges that require tight feedback loops between CAD, machining, prototyping, and real-world validation. Whether optimizing robotic hardware for strength and manufacturability or developing surgical devices for clinical use, I prioritize reliability, robustness, performance optimization, refined mechanical design, and product esthetic throughout the development process.
